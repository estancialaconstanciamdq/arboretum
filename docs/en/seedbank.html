Requesting or exchanging seeds

As part of the botanic-garden network (ArbNet accreditation and BGCI registration), we make our collection available to institutions, researchers and restoration projects through an Index Seminum (a list of seeds available for exchange). To request material, propose an exchange or donate seeds of regional species, get in touch via our contact page.
