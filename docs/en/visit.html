The Arboretum at Estancia La Constancia is a living, 33-hectare botanical garden with more than 30,000 plants and 300 species, in Mar del Plata. We welcome visits by advance booking, so that each group enjoys a personalized, safe and highly educational experience.

How to visit us

Visits are arranged by advance booking. You write to us, together we define the type of tour, the date and the details, and we confirm everything before the day. Since these are outdoor activities, when booking we agree on a main date and an alternative in case the weather does not cooperate.

Before you come

To make the most of the outdoor tour, we recommend:

  • 👟 Comfortable, closed shoes suitable for walking on natural trails.
  • 🧢 Sun protection: cap, sunscreen and sunglasses.
  • 💧 Water to stay hydrated during the walk.
  • 🧥 A jacket or windbreaker depending on the season.
  • A camera or binoculars if you enjoy birdwatching.

Frequently asked questions

NoteDo I need to book? How do I do it?

Yes, all visits are by advance booking. You can book from the experiences page, complete the educational visits form, or write to us directly by email or WhatsApp (see below).

NoteHow long does the visit last?

It depends on the proposal: visits usually last between 2 and 4 hours. When arranging the booking we define the duration according to the group and its goals.

NoteWhat happens if it rains?

When booking we agree on a main date and an alternative. If the weather does not cooperate on the main day, we move to the backup date at no extra cost.

NoteHow much does it cost?

The price depends on the type of visit and the group size. Write to us and we will send you a tailored proposal.

NoteIs there parking?

Yes, we coordinate access and parking when confirming your booking. If you are coming by bus or van, let us know in advance so we can organize entry.

NoteIs the tour accessible?

The arboretum is a natural environment with dirt and grass trails. If your group includes people with reduced mobility, tell us when booking and we will adapt the route as much as possible.

NoteCan I bring my pet?

As this is a conservation area with native wildlife, please check with us before your visit to learn the conditions.

NoteCan I come with children?

Of course! We design experiences for all ages, from early years to adults. Tell us the ages in the group and we will adapt the proposal.
