The Arboretum at Estancia La Constancia opens its doors to educational institutions, teachers and school groups interested in learning directly in contact with nature.

Through guided tours, hands-on activities and ecological observation, we foster a living understanding of biodiversity and the local ecosystems.

TipWhat is an arboretum?

An arboretum is a living, documented collection of trees and woody plants, grown for scientific, educational and conservation purposes: in essence, a “living museum” of trees. Learn more »

Note

Schools that have already lived the experience: Colegio Newton · Colegio Naciones Unidas · Colegio San José de Balcarce.

School visits

Duration: 3–4 hours Level: Primary / Secondary Minimum number of students: 30 students

Designed to complement curricular content in natural sciences, biology and environmental education.

Visit content

Each tour is adapted to the educational level and pedagogical goals of the institution, integrating observation, exploration and learning activities in direct contact with nature.

Some of the topics we cover

  • History and transformation of the landscape: how a former field became a 33-hectare arboretum with more than 30,000 plants.

  • Biodiversity and conservation: recognizing native and exotic species, and the role of botanical gardens and arboreta in protecting flora.

  • Ecology and how ecosystems work: relationships between plants, animals and the environment.

  • Botany in action: observing leaves, flowers, seeds, bark and other plant structures to understand how scientists identify and study species.

  • Environmental education and sustainability: reflecting on caring for the environment, ecological restoration and the importance of individual and collective action.

  • Sensory exploration of nature: activities to discover the sounds, colours, shapes and scents of the garden through playful, participatory proposals.

Methodology

Visits combine guided walks, scientific observation, educational games, teamwork and moments for reflection, fostering curiosity, critical thinking and a bond with nature.

General information

Educational visits at the Arboretum

We offer a living learning space designed to complement your institution’s teaching program. To ensure a safe, well-organized and highly educational experience, all our visits are by advance booking only.

General booking conditions

  • Weather guarantee: when arranging the visit, we set two dates — a main one and an alternative in case of rain.

  • Confirmation: the activity slot is secured through payment of the corresponding deposit.

  • Tailored design: once the booking is requested, our team will contact you to align the teaching objectives with the group’s educational level.

The path to the experience (step by step)

Enrolling your school group is very simple. Just follow these steps:

  • Send your request: complete our web form expressing your interest and tentative dates.

  • Pedagogical coordination: we get in touch to define the teaching objectives and logistics.

  • Date confirmation: the deposit is paid and the dates are locked in (main and alternative).

Enjoy the visit! On the day, your group will live the guided experience in nature.
